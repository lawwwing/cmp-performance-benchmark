Journal Visit

Morning on the ridge

The trail leaves the pines and opens onto a wide ridge. On a clear morning the valley is still in shadow while the upper slope is already warm.

Northwind keeps a small cabin at the trailhead. Water, maps, and a stove are there from May through October. The ridge itself stays open the rest of the year.

Come up for the sunrise if you can. The path is graded, the cabin is staffed on weekends, and the view west from the last switchback is the one on this page.
